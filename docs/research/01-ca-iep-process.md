# The California IEP process, event by event

**As of 2026-10-13.** A California IEP runs as nine events, from first concern to exit. Almost every event has a legal clock, and the clock starts only when something is put in writing.

Citations tagged `Unverified` come from general knowledge of California law and were not checked against the official text. The deadlines in the master timeline that cite EC 56043 were checked against [Disability Rights California](https://www.disabilityrightsca.org/publications/special-education-timelines) and the [F3 Law 2026 chart](https://www.f3law.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/F3-015-CA-SPED-Timelines-2026.pdf).

## How the system fits together

Federal law (IDEA) sets the floor; California Education Code sections 56000 and following add stricter duties ([CDE IEP resources](https://www.cde.ca.gov/sp/se/iepresources.asp)). States can add to IDEA but not subtract from it.

- **LEA (local educational agency):** the district or charter legally responsible for the student. It runs the IEP and sends an administrator to every meeting.
- **SELPA (Special Education Local Plan Area):** the regional consortium every California LEA belongs to. Sets shared procedures, forms, data systems and regional programs ([CDE SELPA list](https://www.cde.ca.gov/sp/se/caselpas.asp)).
- **CDE Special Education Division:** monitors compliance and investigates state complaints.
- **Office of Administrative Hearings (OAH):** runs mediation and due process hearings.

| Role | Who it covers | Core job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| School front office | Principal, office manager, special ed coordinator, SELPA program specialist, case manager (admin side) | Log requests, run the clocks, issue notices, schedule meetings, keep records, report to CALPADS |
| Teacher | General ed teacher, special ed teacher (case manager), SLP, OT, school psychologist | Document need, assess, write goals, deliver services, monitor progress |
| Student | The child; an active planner from 16; the rights holder at 18 | Participate, self-advocate, lead transition planning |
| Family | Parents, guardians, educational rights holders, surrogate parents | Refer, consent, participate as equal team members, monitor, escalate |

## Master timeline

"Days" are calendar days unless noted; days between school sessions and vacations longer than five school days don't count.

| Event | Trigger | Deadline | Authority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Assessment plan sent to family | Written referral received | 15 days | EC 56043(a), 56321 |
| Family decides on assessment plan | Plan received | At least 15 days | EC 56043(b) |
| Assessment done and IEP meeting held | Signed consent received | 60 days | EC 56043(c), (f)(1), 56344 |
| End-of-year referral | Referral 30 or fewer days before year end | Within 30 days of next school year | EC 56043(f) |
| IEP developed after eligibility | Eligibility found | 30 days (federal) | 34 CFR 300.323(c)(1) |
| Services start | Parent consent to IEP | As soon as possible | EC 56043(i) |
| Parent-requested IEP meeting | Written request | 30 days | EC 56043(l), 56343.5 |
| Copies of records to parent | Request | 5 business days, and before any IEP meeting or resolution session | EC 56043(n), 56504 |
| Records to new district | New LEA request | 5 business days | EC 56043(o) |
| Progress reports | Ongoing | At least as often as report cards | EC 56345(a)(3) `Unverified` |
| Annual review | Last IEP | Every 12 months | EC 56343(d) `Unverified` |
| Triennial reassessment | Last eligibility assessment | Every 3 years | EC 56381 `Unverified` |

| Rights transfer notice | Age | 1 year before 18 | EC 56041.5 `Unverified` |

## Event 0: Child Find and pre-referral

The district must find students who may have a disability. Interventions (MTSS, Student Study Team) can run alongside an evaluation but can't delay one ([OSEP Memo 11-07](https://sites.ed.gov/idea/idea-files/osep-memo-11-07-response-to-intervention-rti-memo/)).

- **Front office:** keep one intake log across every channel (email, letter, conversation, SST notes). Any parent ask for "testing" or "evaluation" is a referral and starts Event 1.
- **Teacher:** run tiered interventions, collect data, pass outside diagnoses to the case manager promptly, refer when data shows a suspected disability.
- **Student:** takes part; older students say what helps.
- **Family:** share outside diagnoses in writing; ask for intervention data. A medical diagnosis doesn't by itself change school eligibility.

## Event 1: Written referral

A dated written request starts the 15-day clock; a hallway conversation doesn't. The district answers with an assessment plan, or with Prior Written Notice explaining a refusal (EC 56029, 56321 `Unverified`).

- **Family:** email the special ed director/coordinator and principal; ask for "a full special education assessment in all areas of suspected disability"; attach outside reports; request records at the same time; keep a log.
- **Front office:** date-stamp, enter in the data system, send procedural safeguards ([CDE Parents' Rights](https://www.cde.ca.gov/sp/se/pssummary.asp)), route to case manager and psychologist, arrange interpreter. If declining, issue Prior Written Notice.
- **Teacher:** send intervention data and work samples within days; forward any parent request the same day.
- **Foster youth:** the person who signs must hold educational rights; otherwise the district appoints a surrogate (EC 56050 `Unverified`).

## Event 2: Assessment plan and consent

No testing until a parent signs; the 60-day clock starts when the signed plan comes back. The plan must be in the parent's language and list areas and assessors (EC 56321 `Unverified`).

- **Front office:** send within 15 days; translate; follow up around day 10; date-stamp the return; compute the IEP due date and book the team.
- **Assessors:** cover every area of suspected disability; add an FBA if behavior is a concern.
- **Family:** ask for missing areas in writing before signing; partial consent is allowed.

## Event 3: Assessment

Multiple non-discriminatory measures; a written report per area; no single test decides eligibility (EC 56320, 56327 `Unverified`).

- **Front office:** set internal deadlines a week before the meeting; chase rating scales; offer reports early; send written meeting notice with purpose, time, place and attendees (EC 56341.5 `Unverified`).
- **Assessors:** psychologist (cognitive, social-emotional, behavior), special ed teacher (academics), SLP/OT as listed; English learners assessed so language isn't mistaken for disability ([CDE EL guide](https://www.cde.ca.gov/sp/se/elpracguideswd.asp)).
- **Family:** complete rating scales; provide private evaluations, which must be considered.

## Event 4: Initial IEP meeting

The team, parent as an equal member, decides: is there a qualifying disability, and does it require special education? A diagnosis alone answers neither.

- **Required team (EC 56341 `Unverified`):** parent, general ed teacher, special ed teacher/provider, LEA administrator, someone to interpret results, the student when appropriate.
- **Categories:** autism, deaf-blindness, deafness, emotional disturbance, hearing impairment, intellectual disability, multiple disabilities, orthopedic impairment, other health impairment, specific learning disability, speech or language impairment, traumatic brain injury, visual impairment; California adds established medical disability for ages 3 to 5.
- **IEP contents (EC 56345 `Unverified`):** present levels; measurable goals and progress reporting; services with frequency, location and duration; modifications; least restrictive environment; state testing participation ([CDE alternate assessment guidance](https://www.cde.ca.gov/ta/tg/ca/caaiepteamrev.asp)); special factors; extended school year; transition from 16.
- **Front office:** confirm attendance or written excusals; interpreter and translated documents; audio recording with 24 hours' notice (EC 56341.1(g) `Unverified`); give the parent the IEP and Prior Written Notice.
- **Family:** ask for a draft first; bring a one-page list of priorities; no need to sign at the table.

## Event 5: Consent and implementation

Services start only after consent, then as soon as possible. Partial consent: implement the agreed parts (EC 56346(e) `Unverified`).

- **Front office:** record consent and components; enter in the data system; get the IEP to every responsible teacher and log receipt; set schedules and transport; diary annual and triennial dates.
- **Teacher:** read the whole IEP; log every session; start goal data collection on day one.

## Event 6: Ongoing services, progress and behavior

Progress reports at least as often as report cards; reconvene whenever progress stalls (EC 56343(b) `Unverified`). Behavior plans start from an FBA. After an emergency intervention, notify the parent within a school day and schedule an IEP meeting within 2 days (EC 56521.1 `Unverified`).

- **Front office:** monthly scheduled-vs-delivered service minutes check; process amendments (with or without a meeting by written agreement, 34 CFR 300.324(a)(4) `Unverified`); file behavioral emergency reports.
- **Family:** compare reports to goals; request a meeting in writing if numbers don't move.

## Event 7: Annual review and triennial

Every 12 months; reassessment every 3 years unless both sides agree in writing it's unnecessary (EC 56343(d), 56381 `Unverified`).

- **Front office:** rolling 90-day report of upcoming dates; triennial plans out 60 to 90 days early. An overdue annual or triennial is a monitored compliance violation (see [04](04-buyer-selpa-director.md)).

## Special situations

| Situation | Rule | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Transfer within SELPA | Continue existing IEP | EC 56325(a)(2) `Unverified` |
| Transfer from another CA SELPA | Comparable services up to 30 days, then adopt or rewrite | EC 56325(a)(1) `Unverified` |
| Transfer from another state | Comparable services until assessed and a new IEP is written | EC 56325(a)(3) `Unverified` |
| Discipline | Removals beyond 10 school days need a manifestation determination; services continue | [20 U.S.C. 1415(k)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/20/1415) |
| Discipline before eligibility | If a parent expressed concern in writing or asked for an evaluation first, IDEA protections apply | [20 U.S.C. 1415(k)(5)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/20/1415) |
| Independent study | IEP team decides if it's appropriate | [CDE AB 181 letter](https://www.cde.ca.gov/sp/se/om081922.asp) |
| Parent-placed private school | District must find and assess; equitable services only | [CDE equitable services](https://www.cde.ca.gov/sp/se/ideaeqservices.asp) |
| Placement dispute | Student stays in last agreed placement | [CDE Parents' Rights](https://www.cde.ca.gov/sp/se/pssummary.asp) |

## Event 8: Transition and exit

Exit is a formal team decision with written notice, never a quiet stop.

| Exit route | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| No longer eligible | Reassessment, IEP meeting, Prior Written Notice (34 CFR 300.305(e) `Unverified`) |
| Regular diploma | No reassessment; Summary of Performance |
| Ages out | Through 21; students turning 22 Jan–Jun may finish the year (EC 56026(c)(4) `Unverified`) |
| Revocation of consent | Prior Written Notice; services stop; district can't override (34 CFR 300.300(b)(4) `Unverified`) |
| Leaves district | Records transfer |

Transition planning: measurable postsecondary goals in the IEP in effect at 16 (EC 56345.1 `Unverified`); rights-transfer notice a year before 18 (EC 56041.5 `Unverified`); WorkAbility I and Department of Rehabilitation services ([CDE secondary transition](https://www.cde.ca.gov/sp/se/secondarytransition.asp)).

## Disputes

| Route | Best for | Key facts |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IEP meeting / SELPA ADR | Most disagreements | Fastest; written request starts a 30-day clock |
| Independent Educational Evaluation | Disagreeing with a district assessment | One at public expense per disputed assessment; district funds it or files for due process ([CDE Parents' Rights](https://www.cde.ca.gov/sp/se/pssummary.asp)) |
| State complaint | A broken rule (missed timeline, services not delivered) | CDE decides within 60 days; violations within 1 year ([CDE complaint process](https://www.cde.ca.gov/sp/se/cmplntproc.asp)) |
| Mediation | Services, placement, eligibility | Voluntary; via OAH |
| Due process | Denial of FAPE | OAH judge; 2-year filing window |

## Lessons from two parent threads

Both families were stuck for the same reason: requests made verbally or to the wrong person, with no written response to start a legal clock.

| What happened | Thread | Where it breaks | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Diagnosis paperwork sat unread ~2 months | 1 | Event 0 | Log outside reports on receipt; route to case manager |
| IEP request answered at the door, then silence | 1 | Event 1 | Written request to coordinator; front office answers in writing |
| Psychologist asked for records with no explanation | 1 | Events 1–2 | Assessment plan with safeguards, or Prior Written Notice |
| Behavior plan proposed by phone | 1 | Event 6 | IEP meeting, FBA first, written notice |
| Parent wanted autism "added" to a speech-only IEP | 1 | Event 4 | Diagnosis goes in health info; eligibility change needs reassessment |
| Told an IEP "would not save him" | 2 | Event 1 | Suspected disability triggers Child Find; decline only with PWN |
| 504 accommodations never reached teachers | 2 | Event 5 | Distribute to each teacher; log receipt |
| Prior IEP lost across state moves | 2 | Special situations | Request records from prior districts |
| Kinship caregiver unsure who can sign | 2 | Event 1 | Identify rights holder or surrogate first |
| Expulsion threatened | 2 | Special situations | Manifestation determination applies to IEP and 504 students; request FBA |

**For the product:** intake requests arrive through many channels and nobody owns the clock.
