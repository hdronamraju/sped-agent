# Public data on district compliance

**As of 2026-10-13.** Question asked: which California districts are rated Needs Assistance, Needs Intervention or Needs Substantial Intervention?

## Short answer: no public list

CDE sends each district its annual determination in a letter to the superintendent and doesn't post the results ([CDE letter, annotated by El Dorado Charter SELPA](https://charterselpa.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/Annotated-Annual-Determination-Letter-with-Sample-Data-Sheet-for-23.24-Monitoring.pdf)). States must publicly report district performance on the federal indicators, but don't have to publish the ratings ([IDEA Data Center](https://www.ideadata.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/2018-05/Handout_C2_Hayes_DeRuvo_Wise_LEA_Determinations.pdf)).

## What is public

| Source | What it gives | Link |
| --- | --- | --- |
| California 2026 federal report | 150 LEAs were in Intensive monitoring in FFY 2024 (not named); 13 newly significantly disproportionate in 2025; 14 with ongoing identification struggling to reach compliance | [ED, 2026 SPP/APR CA](https://sites.ed.gov/idea/idea-files/2026-spp-apr-and-state-determination-letters-part-b-california/) |
| Significantly disproportionate LEAs, by year (2021–2024) | Named list with SELPA and area; these LEAs are in intensive monitoring and reserve 15% of IDEA funds | [CDE 2024 list](https://www.cde.ca.gov/sp/se/qa/sigdisplea2024.asp) |
| Annual Performance Report Measures | Per-LEA indicator data the ratings are built on | [CDE](https://www.cde.ca.gov/sp/se/leadatarpts.asp) |
| District board materials | Some districts disclose status, e.g. SCUSD's "CDE Notification of Continued Non-Compliance" (Dec 2024) | [SCUSD deck](https://scusd.edu/sites/main/files/file-attachments/11.4_special_education_update_12-19-24.pptx_1.pdf) |
| CALPADS monitoring report 16.21 | Overdue annual and triennial meetings (restricted to LEA users, not public) | [CALPADS Flash #314](https://www.cde.ca.gov/ds/sp/cl/calpadsupdflash314.asp) |

Examples from the 2024–25 significantly disproportionate list (partial, seen in search results only): ABC Unified, Alhambra Unified, Arcadia Unified, and several El Dorado County Charter SELPA charter schools ([CDE 2024 list](https://www.cde.ca.gov/sp/se/qa/sigdisplea2024.asp)). The direct page returned errors when fetched; pull the full list manually.

## How to get the full ratings

1. **California Public Records Act request to CDE's Special Education Division** for all LEA annual determination letters and monitoring tier assignments for the latest year. CDE pulled the data after the Dec 12, 2025 certification to issue letters in early February 2026 ([CALPADS Flash #314](https://www.cde.ca.gov/ds/sp/cl/calpadsupdflash314.asp)). Likely the single best go-to-market data asset.
2. **Records requests to individual districts or SELPAs** if CDE pushes back.
3. **Rebuild a risk score** from the per-LEA Annual Performance Report Measures.
